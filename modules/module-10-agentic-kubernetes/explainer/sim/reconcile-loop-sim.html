Desired state (the XR spec, in git)

kind: XAppConfig metadata: name: checkout-service spec: appName: checkout-service replicas: 3

Observed state (what's actually running)

kind: ConfigMap data: appName: checkout-service replicas: "3"

Reconcile loop vs one-shot apply

terraform
One-shot: apply makes reality match the plan once. If something drifts an hour later, nothing notices until a human runs plan again.
crossplane
Continuous: the controller compares desired vs observed on every tick, forever, and corrects drift the moment it sees it, no human has to remember to re-run anything.
Self-contained, no network calls. "Ticks" here are simulated steps, not real wall-clock time, the point is the shape of the loop, not its exact interval. Part of Module 10: Agentic Kubernetes and Platform IaC, Agentic Infrastructure as Code.
